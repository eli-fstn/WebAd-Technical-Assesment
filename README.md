# Front-End Technical Assessment:

**Central Student Government - Committee on Website Administration**

Thank you for applying to the Committee on Website Administration. This assessment is meant to be open and relaxed. We want to see how you think, what you choose to build, and how well you understand your own work.

---

## The Task

Build a **single landing page**.

The topic, the look, and the content are entirely up to you.

---

## Creative Freedom

There are no requirements for this task. You decide:

- What the landing page is about (a product, a person, a cause, a made-up brand, anything)
- The color scheme and overall style
- The layout, fonts, and animations
- The tech stack, whether plain HTML/CSS/JS or any framework or library you like

Let your creativity explore. Build something you would be proud to explain.

---

## Use of AI

You may use AI for **limited purposes only**, such as:

- Researching ideas or concepts
- Looking up syntax
- Debugging errors

You may **not** use AI to write your landing page for you.

---

## Panel Explanation

You will present your work to the panel and explain it in your own words. Expect questions such as:

- Why did you choose this topic, design, or tech stack?
- How does this part of your code work?
- Why did you write it this way instead of another way?
- What problems did you run into, and how did you solve them?

---

## Disqualification

You will be **disqualified** if:

- Your code appears to have been written by AI, **or**
- You cannot explain your own code to the panel

Honest, simple work that you understand is always better than impressive work you cannot explain.

---

Best of luck, applicants!
